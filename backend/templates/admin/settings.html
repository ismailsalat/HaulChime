Backup & restore

A backup is a single JSON file containing every lead, partner and activity record. Download one regularly (and always before a redeploy on ephemeral storage). Restoring is additive — existing leads and partners are kept, not overwritten.

Download full backup (JSON) Export leads (CSV) Export audit log (CSV)

CSV is for spreadsheets and is not restorable. Use the JSON backup to move or recover your data.

Audit & retention

Audit records are append-only and kept forever by default. Retention is controlled by the AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS variable and the retention.py script — see backend/LOGGING.md.
